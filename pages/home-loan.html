Tax Savings

Tax Benefits on Your Home Loan

Home loans come with significant tax advantages under the Income Tax Act, 1961. Save up to ₹3.5 Lakhs per year on taxes through two key sections.

Section 80C
₹1.5 Lakh
Maximum Deduction Per Financial Year

Claim deduction on principal repayment of your home loan under Section 80C of the Income Tax Act, up to a limit of ₹1.5 Lakhs per year.

  • Applicable on principal component of EMI
  • Property must not be sold within 5 years of possession
  • Stamp duty & registration charges also deductible (once)
  • Available under old tax regime only
  • Both salaried and self-employed eligible

Pro Tip: If you and your spouse are co-borrowers, each can claim the ₹1.5L deduction separately — effectively doubling your tax savings to ₹3L under 80C alone.

Section 24(b)
₹2 Lakh
Maximum Deduction Per Financial Year

Claim deduction on interest paid on your home loan under Section 24(b), up to ₹2 Lakhs per year for a self-occupied property. No limit for let-out properties.

  • Up to ₹2L for self-occupied property
  • Unlimited deduction for let-out properties
  • Construction must complete within 5 years
  • Pre-EMI interest deductible in 5 equal instalments
  • Co-borrowers can claim independently

Pro Tip: For a let-out property, you can deduct the full interest paid (no cap), making it an excellent investment with substantial tax benefits.

Combined Annual Tax Benefit: Up to ₹3.5 Lakhs

₹1.5L (Section 80C) + ₹2L (Section 24b) = ₹3.5L deduction. At 30% tax bracket, you save ≈ ₹1,05,000 per year in income tax.

From application to key handover — we've streamlined every step so you can focus on finding your dream home, not paperwork.

Our underwriting team performs a comprehensive credit assessment — evaluating your CIBIL score, repayment history, debt-to-income ratio, and employment stability. Property Legal & Technical Valuation

Once your credit profile is approved, our empanelled legal experts verify the property's title, encumbrance, and legal clearance. Simultaneously, our certified technical valuers visit the property to assess its current market value, construction quality, and structural integrity. 3–5 Working Days

Loan Sanction & Agreement Signing

After successful legal and technical clearance, you'll receive an official Loan Sanction Letter detailing the approved amount, interest rate, tenure, EMI schedule, and all terms and conditions. Review thoroughly — you have 30 days to accept. Post acceptance, the loan agreement is executed digitally (via e-sign) or physically at our nearest branch. Pay the processing fee and submit any remaining documents.

1–2 Working Days

Disbursement & Move In!

Once the loan agreement is signed and all conditions are met, we disburse the loan amount directly to the seller's or builder's account (as per the disbursement schedule for under-construction properties). For ready-to-move properties, full disbursement occurs on registration day. You'll receive the NACH mandate for EMI auto-debit, your loan account details, and a warm welcome to the Easy Loan Services family. 🎉

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions — Home Loan

Got questions? We have answers. Here are the most common queries from our home loan applicants.

1
What is the minimum CIBIL score required for a home loan?
A minimum CIBIL score of 650 is required for a home loan from Easy Loan Services. However, a score of 750 or above significantly improves your chances of approval and helps you secure the lowest interest rates. If your score is below 650, we recommend clearing outstanding dues, reducing credit card utilisation, and waiting 3–6 months before applying.
2
How much home loan can I get on my salary?
Generally, you can get a home loan up to 60 times your net monthly salary. For example, with a net salary of ₹50,000/month, you may be eligible for up to ₹30 Lakhs. Additionally, your total EMI obligations (including all existing loans) should not exceed 50–55% of your net monthly income. Adding a co-applicant (spouse, parents, or siblings) with income can significantly increase your loan eligibility.
3
What is the maximum LTV ratio for a home loan?
As per RBI guidelines: Up to ₹30 Lakhs — 90% LTV (10% down payment); ₹30L–₹75L — 80% LTV (20% down payment); Above ₹75 Lakhs — 75% LTV (25% down payment). Note that LTV is calculated on the lower of the market value or agreement value of the property as assessed by the bank's empanelled valuer.
4
Can I get tax benefits on a home loan?
Yes! Home loans offer significant tax benefits: Section 80C — deduction up to ₹1.5 Lakh per year on principal repayment; Section 24(b) — deduction up to ₹2 Lakh per year on interest paid (self-occupied property); Section 80EEA — additional ₹1.5 Lakh deduction on interest for first-time home buyers (if property value ≤ ₹45L). Total combined benefit can reach ₹5 Lakh per year for first-time buyers. Note: These benefits are available under the Old Tax Regime only.
5
What is a balance transfer home loan and how does it help?
A home loan balance transfer allows you to move your existing home loan from another lender to Easy Loan Services at a lower interest rate. This reduces your monthly EMI and/or overall interest burden. For example, transferring a ₹40L loan from 9.5% to 8.4% (at 15 years remaining) saves approximately ₹7–8 Lakhs in total interest. We also offer a top-up loan along with balance transfer, giving you additional funds for personal needs. Minimal documentation and fast processing — typically within 7 days.
6
How long does the entire home loan approval process take?
The complete timeline: In-principle approval — within 24–48 hours of application; Document verification — 1–2 working days; Property legal & technical check — 3–5 working days; Loan sanction — 1–2 working days; Disbursement — within 24 hours of agreement execution. Total end-to-end: approximately 7–10 working days, subject to timely submission of all required documents and property clearance.
7
Is property insurance mandatory for a home loan?
Property insurance (fire and structure policy) is not legally mandatory but is strongly recommended by most lenders, including us. It protects your asset against fire, flood, earthquake, and other perils. We also recommend a Home Loan Protection Plan (HLPP) — a term insurance policy that covers your outstanding loan amount in case of the borrower's untimely demise, ensuring your family isn't burdened with debt. Neither is compulsory, and we will never force you to buy insurance as a condition for loan approval.
8
Can I prepay or foreclose my home loan? Are there charges?
Yes, you can prepay your home loan partially or foreclose it fully at any time. As per RBI guidelines (circular 2011–2014), there are zero prepayment/foreclosure charges on floating-rate home loans for individual borrowers. For fixed-rate loans, a nominal charge (typically 1–2% of prepaid amount) may apply. Partial prepayment reduces either your EMI or tenure (your choice). We recommend lump-sum prepayments during the early years of the loan as the interest component is higher, leading to maximum savings.
