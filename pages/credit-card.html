The minimum CIBIL score required varies by card type. Our Classic Card accepts scores of 650+, Gold and Shopping cards require 700+, while Platinum, Business, and Travel cards require a score of 750 or higher for the best chances of approval and higher credit limits.

Yes! Any single transaction of ₹2,500 or more can be converted to EMI post-purchase. Cashback is calculated at the end of each billing cycle and automatically credited to your outstanding statement balance within 2–3 working days of the statement generation date. For reward points, they are credited within 48 hours of each eligible transaction and can be redeemed via our app for cashback, vouchers, or flight tickets.

Missing a payment due date will attract a late payment fee (₹250 – ₹1,200 depending on outstanding balance) and interest will be charged at 2.5%–3.5% per month on the outstanding amount. It may also impact your CIBIL score. We recommend setting up auto-pay for the minimum amount due to avoid penalties.

In case of loss or theft, immediately block your card via our mobile app (Cards → Block Card), or call our 24/7 helpline at 1800-XXX-XXXX. You are protected by our Zero Liability Policy — you will not be responsible for any fraudulent transactions reported within 3 days of card loss. A replacement card will be issued within 5 working days.
