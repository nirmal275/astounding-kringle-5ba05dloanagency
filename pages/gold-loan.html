Flexible Repayment

Choose Your Repayment Style

We understand every borrower has unique cash flow needs. That's why we offer three flexible repayment options for our gold loans.

Bullet Repayment

Pay only the interest during the loan tenure and repay the entire principal amount at the end of the loan period in one lump sum. Ideal for traders and seasonal income earners who expect a large cash inflow later.

Best for: Seasonal Income
Monthly EMI

Repay your gold loan in fixed equal monthly instalments comprising both principal and interest. This is the most popular option for salaried individuals or those with a fixed monthly income. EMI auto-debited from your account.

Best for: Salaried Borrowers
Interest-Only Repayment

Pay only the monthly interest component throughout the loan tenure, with the principal repayable at the end. This minimises your monthly outgo and is perfect for businesspeople managing day-to-day operational liquidity.

Best for: Business Owners

For loans above ₹20 Lakhs, additional KYC verification may be required as per RBI guidelines.

We accept gold jewellery from 18K to 24K purity, including necklaces, bangles, rings, earrings, chains, and other ornaments. BIS hallmarked jewellery fetches the highest LTV. We also accept gold coins and bars issued by nationalised banks. We do not accept gold plated items, gold-filled jewellery, or white gold.

Our gold loan process is the fastest in the industry. From the time you walk into our branch with your gold to receiving the loan amount, the entire process takes just 30 to 45 minutes. If you are unable to repay the gold loan within the agreed tenure, we will first send multiple reminders and provide a 30-day grace period. We also offer a loan renewal/extension option. We encourage you to communicate with us early if you're facing financial difficulty.
