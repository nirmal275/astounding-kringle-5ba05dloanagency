FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about car loans — answered simply and clearly.

Our car loan interest rates start from 9.2% per annum on a reducing balance basis for salaried applicants with a strong CIBIL score (750+). The actual rate you're offered depends on factors like your credit score, loan-to-value ratio, income, and the type of vehicle (new vs. used). Self-employed applicants and used car loans typically attract slightly higher rates. We work with multiple lenders to negotiate the best possible rate for your profile.

Yes! For new cars, we offer up to 100% on-road funding, which includes the ex-showroom price, road tax, registration charges, and first-year insurance premium. This means you don't have to pay anything out of pocket to drive your car home. This benefit is available for salaried applicants with a CIBIL score above 720 and income above ₹30,000/month. For used cars, funding is typically up to 80% of the vehicle's current market value.

For new car loans with complete documentation, our standard approval time is 24 hours. Disbursement to the dealer typically happens within 24–48 hours after loan sanction. For used car loans, the process may take up to 48–72 hours due to vehicle inspection and valuation. In urgent cases, we also offer express processing where approval can be granted within 4–6 hours for eligible applicants.

For new car loans, the maximum tenure is 84 months (7 years). For used car loans, the maximum tenure is 60 months (5 years), subject to the vehicle age at the end of the loan not exceeding 10 years. A longer tenure reduces your monthly EMI but increases the total interest paid. Use our EMI Calculator to find the right balance between EMI amount and total cost.

A CIBIL score of 700 or above is generally required for a standard car loan approval. Applicants with scores of 750+ get the best interest rates and higher loan amounts. If your score is between 650–699, approval may still be possible with a co-applicant, a larger down payment, or at a slightly higher interest rate. If your CIBIL score is below 650, we recommend improving it before applying. Contact our credit counsellors for a free credit health assessment.

For new car loans, there is zero foreclosure charge after you have completed 12 EMIs. If you foreclose before 12 months, a charge of 2%–4% of the outstanding principal may apply depending on the lender. For used car loans, a 2% foreclosure charge applies after 12 months. Partial prepayments are also allowed after 6 months, with no penalty for up to 25% of the outstanding principal per year on floating-rate loans.

Absolutely! We provide used car loans for vehicles up to 5 years old at the time of application. The car must not be more than 10 years old at the end of the loan tenure. We fund up to 80% of the vehicle's current market value as determined by our empanelled valuers. The vehicle should have a clear RC title with no hypothecation, valid insurance, and no major accident or flood damage history. We accept cars from certified pre-owned dealers as well as private sellers.

Yes! We have a dedicated EV Loan product with an additional interest rate concession of 0.25% compared to equivalent petrol or diesel vehicles. We finance all major EV models including Tata Nexon EV, MG ZS EV, Hyundai IONIQ, BYD Atto 3, Ola Electric S1, and more. Our EV loans cover the full ex-showroom price plus charger installation charges (up to ₹50,000). You can also combine this with applicable FAME-II and state government subsidies to reduce your effective loan amount.
