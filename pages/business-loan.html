Eligibility Criteria

Who Can Apply for a Business Loan?

We've simplified our eligibility criteria to ensure most established businesses qualify. Meeting these basic requirements speeds up your loan approval significantly.

Check My Eligibility
Business Age

Minimum 2 years of continuous business operations with valid proof of establishment and track record.

Annual Turnover

Minimum annual turnover of ₹25 Lakh with consistent revenue growth and audited financial statements.

GST Registration

Active GST registration with at least 1 year of GSTR filings. Regular and timely returns are a plus.

CIBIL Score 700+

Both business and promoter credit scores above 700. Higher scores attract better rates and higher limits.

Applicant Age

Proprietor / Director must be between 21 – 65 years of age at the time of loan application.

India Registered

Business must be registered and operating in India — sole proprietorship, partnership, LLP, or Pvt Ltd.

Got questions about business loans? We've got clear, straightforward answers.

Your business must be at least 2 years old with continuous operations and verifiable financial history. Startups under 2 years may be eligible under special MSME schemes, government startup loans, or if the promoter has a strong personal credit profile. MUDRA loans are also available for newer micro-businesses.

You can get a business loan up to ₹5 Crore depending on your annual turnover, profitability, creditworthiness, and the type of loan. Unsecured business loans are generally capped at ₹75 Lakh, while secured or term loans can go higher based on collateral and financials.

No collateral is required for unsecured business loans up to ₹75 Lakh if you have a good credit score and strong financials. For larger loans (above ₹75 Lakh), collateral in the form of business property, plant, or equipment may be required. MSME loans under CGTMSE scheme also offer credit guarantee without collateral.

Our digital process enables approval within 24 hours and full disbursement within 48 hours of document submission for eligible applicants. The speed depends on the completeness of your documents, the loan amount, and your credit profile. Larger loan amounts may take 3–5 business days for additional due diligence.

A minimum CIBIL score of 700 for both the business and the promoter is recommended. Scores above 750 attract lower interest rates and higher loan amounts. If your score is between 650–700, you may still qualify with additional documentation or by providing collateral. Scores below 650 generally result in rejection or very high rates.

Yes, you can prepay your business loan partially or fully. Prepayment charges typically apply after a lock-in period of 6 months and range from 2% – 4% of the outstanding principal. After 24 months of repayment, prepayment is often allowed without any charges. Specific terms depend on your loan agreement.

Absolutely! Sole proprietors, partnership firms, LLPs, and private limited companies are all eligible to apply for a business loan. Sole proprietors need to provide their personal PAN, Aadhaar, and business proof. The loan assessment considers both personal and business financials for sole proprietorships.

Missing an EMI results in a late payment penalty of 2% – 3% per month on the overdue amount. It also negatively impacts your CIBIL score. If payments are missed for 90+ days, the account may be classified as an NPA (Non-Performing Asset).
