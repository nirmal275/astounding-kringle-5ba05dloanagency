Welcome to Easy Loan Services' Privacy Policy. This document governs all personal data interactions between you (the user) and Easy Loan Services Pvt. Ltd. ("Company," "we," "us," or "our"). By accessing our website www.easyloanservices.in or submitting a loan application, you consent to the practices described herein. Please read this policy carefully before using our services.
Section 01

Information We Collect

Easy Loan Services collects various categories of information to provide you with accurate, efficient, and personalised loan services. The data we collect falls into four primary categories: personal identification data, financial and credit data, technical and device data, and usage data generated through your interaction with our platform. Each category is described in detail below to ensure complete transparency about our data collection practices.

Personal Identification Information: When you register an account, fill out a loan application, or contact our support team, we collect information that identifies you as an individual. This includes your full legal name, date of birth, residential and correspondence address, PAN card number, Aadhaar number (as permitted under applicable law), passport details (where provided), voter identification number, email address, and mobile phone number. We may also collect photographs and signatures required for KYC (Know Your Customer) compliance under the Prevention of Money Laundering Act, 2002, and guidelines issued by the Reserve Bank of India (RBI).

Financial and Credit Information: To assess your loan eligibility and determine appropriate loan amounts and interest rates, we collect comprehensive financial data. This includes your employment status, employer name and address, monthly and annual income, salary slips for the preceding three to six months, Income Tax Returns (ITR) for the preceding two to three financial years, Form 16, bank account statements, details of existing loans and credit facilities, credit card usage information, outstanding debts, and your credit score obtained from authorised Credit Information Companies (CICs) such as CIBIL, Experian, CRIF High Mark, and Equifax. For self-employed applicants, we additionally collect business registration documents, audited financial statements, balance sheets, and profit and loss accounts.

Device and Technical Information: When you visit our website or use our mobile application, our servers automatically record technical information about your session. This includes your Internet Protocol (IP) address, browser type and version, operating system, device model and manufacturer, screen resolution, referring URL, pages visited within our platform, time and date of access, session duration, geographic location (derived from IP address or GPS coordinates if you grant location permission), and unique device identifiers such as IMEI or UDID. This information is primarily used for platform security, fraud prevention, and optimising the user experience.

Cookies and Tracking Data: We use cookies, web beacons, pixel tags, local storage, and similar tracking technologies to collect data about your browsing behaviour on our website. This includes pages you visit, links you click, features you use, forms you partially complete, and the sequence of your interactions. This data helps us personalise content, remember your preferences, measure the effectiveness of our marketing campaigns, and improve the overall performance of our digital platforms. You can manage cookie preferences through your browser settings or our Cookie Consent Manager; however, disabling certain cookies may limit the functionality of our services.

Section 02

How We Use Your Information

The information we collect is used exclusively for legitimate, clearly defined purposes in connection with the provision of our loan services, regulatory compliance, and the continuous improvement of our platform. We do not use your personal data for any purpose that is incompatible with the purposes described herein unless we obtain your separate, explicit consent or are required to do so by applicable law. All processing activities are carried out on the basis of one or more lawful grounds, including contractual necessity, legal obligation, legitimate interest, and your informed consent.

Loan Processing and Underwriting: Your personal, financial, and credit data is primarily used to evaluate and process your loan application. We use this information to verify your identity, assess your creditworthiness, determine your debt-to-income ratio, calculate your loan eligibility, select suitable lending partners from our network, generate personalised loan offers, and facilitate the disbursal of approved loans into your designated bank account. Our underwriting algorithms may combine multiple data points to produce a risk profile that informs lending decisions, subject always to human review and regulatory guidelines.

Communication and Customer Support: We use your contact information to send you important communications related to your loan application, including application acknowledgment, document verification updates, loan approval or rejection notices, loan agreement copies, EMI reminders, payment receipts, and account statements. We may also contact you via email, SMS, WhatsApp, or phone call to provide customer support, respond to your queries, and offer guidance throughout the loan lifecycle. You may opt out of non-essential marketing communications at any time by clicking the "unsubscribe" link in our emails or contacting our support team.

Analytics, Research, and Service Improvement: Aggregated and anonymised data derived from user interactions is used to analyse trends, measure platform performance, conduct internal research, test new features, and improve our products and services. We may also use your behavioural data to personalise your experience on our platform — for example, by showing you loan products most relevant to your profile, pre-filling application forms with information you have previously provided, or displaying targeted content based on your stated preferences. These activities are carried out under our legitimate interest to provide a better, more relevant user experience.

Fraud Prevention and Legal Compliance: We process your data to detect, investigate, and prevent fraudulent activity, money laundering, identity theft, and other illegal activities. As a regulated financial intermediary, we are required by law to conduct mandatory due diligence under AML/CFT (Anti-Money Laundering / Combating the Financing of Terrorism) regulations, maintain records for prescribed periods under the Income Tax Act and Companies Act, respond to lawful requests from government authorities, courts, and regulatory bodies such as the Reserve Bank of India and the Securities and Exchange Board of India.

Section 03

Information Sharing

We do NOT sell your personal data. Easy Loan Services does not sell, rent, lease, or otherwise trade your personal or financial information to third parties for their own marketing or commercial purposes.

While we are committed to never selling your data, there are specific, limited circumstances under which we share your information with trusted third parties. All such sharing is governed by written data processing agreements that require these parties to protect your information in accordance with applicable law and our internal security standards. We only share the minimum necessary information required for the specific purpose and impose strict limitations on how recipients may use the data.

Lending Partners and Financial Institutions: When you submit a loan application, your information is shared with the Banks, Non-Banking Financial Companies (NBFCs), Housing Finance Companies (HFCs), and Microfinance Institutions (MFIs) within our lending network who may be able to fulfil your loan request. This sharing is essential for processing your application and is covered by your express consent provided at the time of application. Our lending partners are themselves regulated entities subject to RBI guidelines and are independently responsible for compliance with applicable data protection laws.

Credit Information Companies (CICs): To evaluate your creditworthiness, we are authorised by your consent to obtain your credit report from registered CICs including TransUnion CIBIL, Experian India, CRIF High Mark, and Equifax India. In accordance with the Credit Information Companies (Regulation) Act, 2005, a "hard inquiry" may be recorded on your credit file each time we access your report in relation to a formal loan application. We also report the performance of disbursed loans to CICs on a monthly basis as required by regulation.

Service Providers and Technology Partners: We engage carefully vetted third-party service providers who assist us in operating our platform and delivering our services. These include cloud hosting providers (such as Amazon Web Services and Google Cloud Platform), payment gateway operators, KYC verification technology companies, SMS and email communication providers, data analytics firms, and cybersecurity specialists. These parties act as Data Processors on our behalf and are contractually prohibited from using your information for any purpose other than providing the specific service we have contracted them for.

Legal and Regulatory Disclosure: We may disclose your information when required by law, judicial order, or government regulation, or where we believe in good faith that disclosure is necessary to protect the safety of any person, to prevent fraud, or to protect the rights and property of Easy Loan Services. In such cases, we will take reasonable steps to notify you of the disclosure unless we are legally prohibited from doing so. Any information shared in these circumstances will be limited strictly to what is required.

Business Transfers: In the event of a merger, acquisition, restructuring, sale of assets, or similar corporate transaction, your personal data may be transferred to the successor entity as part of the business. You will be notified via email and a prominent notice on our website at least 30 days before your data becomes subject to a different privacy policy as a result of such a transaction.

Section 04

Data Security

Easy Loan Services implements a comprehensive, multi-layered information security framework to protect your personal and financial data from unauthorised access, disclosure, alteration, loss, or destruction. Our security practices are aligned with internationally recognised standards including ISO/IEC 27001:2022 and the guidelines issued by the Reserve Bank of India on Information Security, Electronic Banking, Technology Risk Management, and Cyber Frauds. We maintain a dedicated Information Security team that continuously monitors our systems, conducts regular vulnerability assessments, and responds to emerging threats.

Encryption and Transmission Security: All data transmitted between your device and our servers is encrypted using industry-standard Transport Layer Security (TLS) protocol, version 1.2 or higher, with strong cipher suites. Our website uses an Extended Validation (EV) SSL certificate, indicated by the padlock icon in your browser's address bar. Data stored in our databases is encrypted at rest using AES-256 bit encryption. Sensitive fields such as Aadhaar numbers, PAN numbers, and bank account details are additionally tokenised or hashed using one-way cryptographic functions where full-form storage is not required.

Access Controls and Authentication: Access to customer data within our organisation is governed by a strict Role-Based Access Control (RBAC) policy. Only employees and contractors who have a legitimate business need are granted access to specific categories of personal data, and such access is logged and audited. All internal systems are protected by multi-factor authentication (MFA), and privileged access to production databases requires additional approval workflows. Employees undergo mandatory data protection training at the time of onboarding and annually thereafter. All staff members are bound by confidentiality obligations under their employment contracts.

Infrastructure and Application Security: Our platform is hosted on ISO 27001-certified cloud infrastructure with automated backups, distributed denial-of-service (DDoS) protection, and intrusion detection and prevention systems (IDS/IPS). Our web applications are protected by a Web Application Firewall (WAF) that filters malicious traffic. We conduct regular penetration testing and code security reviews, and we operate a responsible disclosure programme for external security researchers. Our mobile applications are subjected to OWASP Mobile Security Testing Guide (MSTG) compliance checks before each release.

Data Breach Notification: In the unlikely event of a personal data breach that is likely to result in a risk to your rights and freedoms, Easy Loan Services will notify the relevant regulatory authority within 72 hours of becoming aware of the breach, as required by applicable law. If the breach is assessed to pose a high risk to you personally, we will also notify you directly via email and/or SMS without undue delay. Our breach notification will describe the nature of the breach, the categories of data affected, the likely consequences, and the measures we are taking to address it.

Section 05

Cookies Policy

A cookie is a small text file that a website stores on your computer or mobile device when you visit it. Cookies are widely used to make websites work more efficiently and to provide information to website owners. Easy Loan Services uses cookies and similar technologies — including web beacons, pixel tags, local storage, and session storage — to operate and improve our digital platforms, deliver relevant content, and analyse user behaviour.

We obtain your consent before placing any non-essential cookies on your device through our Cookie Consent Banner, which appears on your first visit to our website. Essential cookies, which are strictly necessary for the website to function, are deployed without requiring your consent. You may withdraw your consent for non-essential cookies at any time by adjusting your browser settings or returning to our Cookie Preference Centre accessible in the footer of our website. Please note that withdrawing consent may affect the functionality and personalisation of our services.

Types of Cookies We Use: The table below describes the categories of cookies deployed on our platform, their purpose, and their typical retention period:

Category Purpose Examples Duration
Essential Enable core website functions, maintain session state, support security features, CSRF protection JSESSIONID, XSRF-TOKEN, auth_token Session
Functional Remember your preferences, language settings, pre-fill form data, maintain login state across visits user_prefs, lang_setting, remember_me 30 days
Analytics Measure website traffic, track user journeys, identify popular pages, report errors, improve performance _ga (Google Analytics), _hj (Hotjar), _fbp Up to 2 years
Marketing Deliver relevant advertisements on third-party platforms, measure campaign effectiveness, retargeting _gcl_au, _fbp, LinkedIn Insight Tag Up to 90 days

Managing Your Cookie Preferences: Most web browsers allow you to control cookies through their settings menu. You can typically choose to block all cookies, block third-party cookies, or delete cookies that have already been set. For instructions specific to your browser, please visit the help page of your browser (Chrome, Firefox, Safari, Edge, or Opera). Additionally, you can opt out of Google Analytics tracking by installing the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on available at tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout. For interest-based advertising, you may opt out via the Digital Advertising Alliance's opt-out portal at optout.aboutads.info.

Section 06

Your Rights

As an individual whose personal data we process, you are entitled to a number of rights under applicable data protection law, including the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDPA), the Information Technology Act, 2000, and its associated rules. Easy Loan Services is committed to respecting and facilitating the exercise of these rights. You may submit a request to exercise any of the rights described below by contacting our designated Grievance Officer through the contact details provided in Section 10 of this policy.

We will respond to all verified rights requests within 30 calendar days of receipt. In complex cases or where a large volume of requests are received simultaneously, we may extend this period by a further 30 days, and will notify you of such extension within the initial 30-day period. We will not charge a fee for exercising your rights unless your request is manifestly unfounded or excessive, in which case we may charge a reasonable administrative fee or refuse to comply, stating our reasons.

Right to Access

Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you and information about how it is processed.

Right to Rectification

Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete personal data we hold about you without delay.

Right to Erasure

Request deletion of your personal data where it is no longer necessary for the purpose collected.

Right to Portability

Receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format and transfer it to another controller.

Right to Object

Object to processing based on legitimate interests, including profiling for direct marketing.

Right to Human Review

Request human review of any automated decision, including credit scoring, that significantly affects you.

Right to Restriction

Request that we restrict processing of your data in certain circumstances while a dispute is resolved.

Right to Withdraw Consent

Withdraw any consent previously given without affecting processing that occurred prior to withdrawal.

Important Limitation: Certain rights may be limited where we are required to retain data by law (e.g., for regulatory record-keeping), where the data is necessary for the establishment, exercise, or defence of legal claims, or where deletion would impact your ability to make future loan enquiries. We will clearly explain any such limitation when responding to your request.

Our website, mobile application, and communications may contain links to third-party websites, applications, and services — including our lending partners' websites, financial comparison portals, government portals (such as the Income Tax e-Filing portal or DigiLocker), and social media platforms. These links are provided for your convenience and informational purposes only. When you click on a third-party link and leave our platform, you are subject to the privacy policy and terms of service of the destination website or application. We strongly encourage you to review the privacy policy of every website you visit, particularly before providing any personal or financial information. Easy Loan Services cannot be held responsible for any damage or loss arising from your use of third-party websites, whether or not they are linked from our platform.

Our platform may also embed third-party widgets, social sharing buttons, video players, maps, and payment gateways from providers such as Google Maps, YouTube, Razorpay, Paytm, and social media platforms. These embedded components may independently set cookies or collect usage data from you. Please refer to the respective third party's privacy policy for information on their data practices. Where possible, we configure such integrations to use privacy-enhancing modes (such as YouTube's privacy-enhanced embed mode) to minimise unsolicited data collection.

If you discover a broken link or have concerns about a third-party site linked from our platform, please contact us at legal@easyloanservices.in and we will investigate and take appropriate action, which may include removing the link from our platform if we determine that the linked site does not meet acceptable standards of privacy or security.

Section 08

Children's Privacy

Easy Loan Services' loan products, financial services, and digital platforms are intended exclusively for adults who are 18 years of age or older and are legally competent to enter into contracts under the Indian Contract Act, 1872. Our services are not directed at, intended for, or marketed to individuals below the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect, process, or store personal information from minors below the age of 18.

If we become aware that a person under the age of 18 has provided us with personal information without verifiable parental or guardian consent, we will take immediate steps to delete that information from our systems and terminate any associated account. If you are a parent or legal guardian and believe that your minor child has inadvertently submitted personal information to us, we urge you to contact our Grievance Officer immediately at grievance@easyloanservices.in or call our helpline at 1800-123-4567. We will investigate the matter and take appropriate corrective action within 48 hours of receiving your notification.

We implement age verification mechanisms at the point of loan application to prevent minors from applying for financial products. These mechanisms include verification of date of birth against government-issued identity documents during KYC processing. In the event that such a document appears to have been fraudulently used to misrepresent age, we reserve the right to reject the application and report the matter to appropriate law enforcement authorities.

For educational and informational content on our blog or resource pages that may be accessed by individuals under 18, we do not engage in behavioural tracking or serve personalised advertisements. If you are under the age of 18, we encourage you to seek the guidance of a parent or guardian before using any financial services platform, including our own.

Section 09

Changes to This Policy

Easy Loan Services reserves the right to update, modify, or revise this Privacy Policy at any time to reflect changes in our business operations, data processing practices, applicable law, or regulatory requirements. All changes to this policy will be effective from the date they are published on our website, unless a different effective date is specified. We are committed to maintaining transparency with our users about any significant changes to the way we handle personal data.

In the event of material changes — defined as changes that significantly alter the way we use your personal data or meaningfully reduce your rights — we will provide you with prominent advance notice. This notice will be delivered via email to the address registered with your account, through a pop-up notification on our website, and/or through a banner or alert message in our mobile application. For material changes, we will provide at least 30 days' notice before the changes take effect, allowing you time to review the updated policy and, if you choose, to exercise your right to withdraw consent or close your account.

We maintain an archive of all prior versions of this Privacy Policy, which is accessible upon request. If you wish to review a previous version, please contact our Grievance Officer. The "Last Updated" date at the top of this policy reflects the date of the most recent revision. We encourage you to review this policy periodically, particularly before submitting a new loan application or providing additional personal information through our platform.

Your continued use of our website, mobile application, or any of our services after the effective date of a revised Privacy Policy constitutes your acknowledgment of the revised terms. If you do not agree with any aspect of the revised policy, you must discontinue use of our services and may request deletion of your account by contacting us through the details provided below. We will process such requests in accordance with your rights under applicable law.

Section 10

Contact Information

If you have any questions, concerns, or requests regarding this Privacy Policy or the way Easy Loan Services handles your personal data, we are here to help. We have appointed a dedicated Grievance Officer and a Data Protection Lead who are responsible for overseeing compliance with our data protection obligations and addressing user concerns in a timely manner.

You may submit a formal privacy request or complaint by writing to our Grievance Officer at the address below. We treat all privacy communications with the utmost seriousness and confidentiality. Please include as much detail as possible in your communication — including your full name, registered email address, the specific data concern you wish to raise, and any relevant reference numbers or application IDs — so that we can locate your records and respond accurately. We will never use information provided in a privacy complaint for any purpose other than addressing the specific concern raised.
