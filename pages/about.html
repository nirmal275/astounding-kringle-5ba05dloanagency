Our Story

15 Years of Empowering Financial Dreams

Easy Loan Services was founded in 2010 in the financial heart of Mumbai, Maharashtra, with a simple but powerful vision — to make loan financing accessible, transparent, and stress-free for every Indian. What started as a small team of passionate financial consultants working out of a single office in Andheri has grown into one of India's most trusted multi-product loan agencies, serving customers across 25+ cities nationwide.

Over the past 15 years, we have witnessed and navigated India's rapidly evolving financial landscape — from the demonetisation era to the digital banking revolution. Throughout every challenge, our commitment has remained unwavering: put the customer first. Our team of over 250 dedicated financial professionals works tirelessly to match each client with the right loan product at the best possible interest rate, ensuring minimal paperwork and maximum convenience.

Today, Easy Loan Services proudly holds RBI registration, NBFC certification, and the prestigious ISO 9001:2015 quality certification — testament to the standards of excellence and governance that define everything we do. We have disbursed over ₹2,500 crore in loans to more than 50,000 families and businesses across India, helping them buy homes, grow businesses, fund education, and realise their deepest aspirations.

  • 250+ financial experts across 25+ cities in India
  • Partnered with 15+ leading banks and NBFCs
  • Digital-first loan processing in as little as 24 hours
  • Dedicated relationship managers for every borrower

Our Mission & Vision
