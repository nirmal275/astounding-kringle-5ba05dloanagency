FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about personal loans at Easy Loan Services — answered clearly.

You can apply for a personal loan starting from as little as ₹50,000 up to a maximum of ₹50,00,000 (₹50 Lakhs). The exact loan amount sanctioned depends on your monthly income, existing EMI obligations, credit score, employment stability, and lender's assessment. Salaried individuals with a higher take-home salary and a good CIBIL score are eligible for higher loan amounts.

Personal loan interest rates at Easy Loan Services start from 10.5% p.a. The actual rate offered to you depends on several factors including your credit score (CIBIL), monthly income, employer category, loan tenure, and relationship with the lender. Applicants with a CIBIL score of 750+ and stable employment history are typically offered the lowest rates. We recommend checking your eligibility and getting a personalised quote before applying.

Once your loan is approved and all documents are verified, the loan amount is disbursed to your bank account within 24 hours. In many cases, especially for pre-approved or existing customers, funds can be credited within a few hours. We use NEFT and IMPS for instant bank transfers. The entire process from application to disbursal typically takes 1–2 business days.

Absolutely not. Personal loans from Easy Loan Services are completely unsecured loans. You do not need to pledge any collateral such as property, gold, fixed deposits, or any other assets. The loan is approved entirely based on your creditworthiness, income, and repayment capacity. This makes personal loans ideal for urgent financial needs without risking your assets.

We require a minimum CIBIL score of 700 for personal loan approval, though a score of 750 or above significantly improves your chances and helps you get better interest rates. If your score is below 700, we recommend taking steps to improve it before applying — such as repaying existing debts, maintaining low credit utilization, and avoiding multiple loan applications simultaneously. You can check your CIBIL score for free before applying.

Yes, you can prepay or foreclose your personal loan after completing a minimum lock-in period (typically 12 EMIs). However, lenders may charge a prepayment penalty ranging from 2%–5% of the outstanding principal. Some lenders offer zero prepayment charges after 24 months. We recommend reviewing your loan agreement for specific prepayment terms. Partial prepayment can also be made to reduce your outstanding balance and either lower your EMI or shorten your tenure.

Transparency is one of our core values. The charges associated with personal loans include:
  • Processing Fee: 1%–3% of loan amount (one-time, deducted from disbursed amount)
  • GST: 18% on processing fee
  • Late Payment Penalty: 2%–3% per month on overdue amount
  • Prepayment Charges: 2%–5% of outstanding principal (after lock-in period)
  • ECS/NACH Bounce Charges: ₹300–₹750 per bounce
There are no hidden charges. All fees are disclosed upfront in your loan agreement.

Yes, self-employed individuals, business owners, freelancers, and professionals (doctors, CAs, architects) can all apply for a personal loan. The eligibility criteria for self-employed applicants require:
  • Minimum 2 years of business existence with stable income
  • ITR filing for at least the last 2 years
  • Good business bank statement with regular turnover
  • CIBIL score of 700+ (personal)
The loan amount for self-employed applicants is determined based on net annual income as shown in ITR.
