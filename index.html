Frequently Asked Questions

Can't find your answer here? Contact our expert team — we're always happy to help.

Most lenders require a minimum CIBIL score of 700-750 for a personal loan. However, at Easy Loan Services, we work with 50+ lenders and can often find solutions even for scores as low as 650. We'll help improve your approval chances through the right lender matching.

Personal loans can be disbursed within 24-48 hours of document verification. In many cases, for salaried individuals with good credit, we can get same-day approval and next-day disbursal. Pre-approved customers often receive funds within 2-4 hours.

Loan amounts vary by type: Personal Loans up to ₹50 Lakhs, Business Loans up to ₹5 Crore, Home Loans up to ₹5 Crore (higher in metro cities), Car Loans up to ₹1.5 Crore, Gold Loans based on gold value. The actual amount depends on your eligibility and lender norms.

Yes! We offer loans for people with low CIBIL scores through our network of NBFCs and alternative lenders. Secured loans (gold, home) are easier to get with low scores. We also provide CIBIL improvement advice to help you qualify for better rates over time.

Prepayment terms vary by lender. Many lenders allow prepayment after 12 EMIs with a nominal charge (1-3% of outstanding amount). We'll clearly explain all prepayment terms before you sign any agreement. Some lenders offer zero prepayment penalty — we'll find the best fit for you.

For a home loan, you typically need: Identity proof (Aadhaar/Passport), address proof, income documents (salary slips or ITR), bank statements for 6 months, property documents (sale deed, approved plan, NOC), and photographs. We'll give you a customized checklist based on your profile.

Our loan advisory and application services are completely FREE for customers. We are compensated by the lenders we work with. There is no fee for consultation, application, CIBIL check, or document assistance. You only pay the standard processing fee that you would pay directly to the bank anyway.

FOIR (Fixed Obligation to Income Ratio) is the percentage of your income that goes toward existing EMIs. Most lenders cap this at 40-50%. For example, if you earn ₹50,000/month and have EMIs of ₹10,000, your FOIR is 20% — you'd qualify for additional EMI of up to ₹15,000 (bringing FOIR to 50%).

Absolutely! Self-employed individuals, business owners, freelancers, and professionals (doctors, CAs, architects) can all get loans. You'll need ITR for 2 years, business proof, and bank statements. Business loans are specifically designed for entrepreneurs. Contact us for tailored solutions.

Once you apply through us, you'll receive regular updates via SMS and email. You can also call or WhatsApp our 24/7 helpline. Your dedicated relationship manager will keep you informed at every step — from application to disbursal.

Personal loan interest rates typically range from 10.5% to 24% p.a. depending on your CIBIL score, income, employer profile, and chosen lender. Salaried employees of reputed companies with 750+ CIBIL scores often get rates starting at 10.5%. We compare multiple lenders to get you the best rate.

EMI (Equated Monthly Installment) is the fixed monthly amount you pay toward your loan. It consists of both principal and interest. It's calculated using the formula: EMI = P × r × (1+r)^n / ((1+r)^n - 1), where P = principal, r = monthly interest rate, n = number of months. Use our EMI calculator for instant results.

Home loan tenures can go up to 30 years, making monthly EMIs very affordable. However, the tenure is also linked to your age — most lenders want the loan to be repaid by your retirement (typically age 60-65). So a 35-year-old applicant might get a maximum tenure of 25-30 years.

Yes! If you've been paying your EMIs regularly for 6-12 months, you may be eligible for a top-up loan. Home loan top-ups are especially popular — you can borrow additional funds at the same (or slightly higher) rate as your existing home loan. Contact us to check your top-up eligibility.

Missing an EMI will attract a late payment penalty (usually 1-2% per month on overdue amount) and will negatively impact your CIBIL score. If you're facing financial difficulty, contact us immediately — we can help negotiate a grace period, restructuring, or moratorium with your lender before you default.

For most personal and car loans, a guarantor or co-applicant is not mandatory if you meet eligibility criteria. However, adding a co-applicant (spouse, parent, sibling) can significantly increase your loan eligibility and may help get a lower interest rate. For home loans, a co-applicant also provides tax benefits.

A home loan balance transfer can save significant interest if the new rate is 0.5%+ lower than your current rate and you have a substantial outstanding amount (typically ₹25 lakhs+) with 10+ years remaining. Calculate your savings against transfer costs (processing fees, stamp duty). We provide free balance transfer analysis.

Fixed rate loans have the same interest rate throughout the tenure — predictable EMIs but typically higher rates. Floating rate loans change with market rates (RBI policy). They start lower but can increase. For short tenures (personal, car loans), fixed rates are predictable. For long tenures (home loans), floating rates are generally better in a falling rate environment.

Home loan borrowers can claim deductions under Section 80C (up to ₹1.5 lakh on principal) and Section 24(b) (up to ₹2 lakh on interest for self-occupied property). Education loans offer deductions under Section 80E. Business loan interest is fully deductible as business expense. No direct tax benefits on personal or car loans.

To maximize your approval chances: (1) Maintain CIBIL score 750+, (2) Keep FOIR below 40%, (3) Have stable employment for 2+ years, (4) Avoid multiple loan applications simultaneously, (5) Add a co-applicant if needed, (6) Provide all income sources in documents, (7) Choose the right lender for your profile — which is exactly what we help with!
